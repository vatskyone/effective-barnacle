## 1. The device at a glance

- **Screen** (top left, set flush into the case with no frame): shows the current mode and an animated picture of the sound. The four coloured values along its bottom edge belong to the four knobs.
- **Four coloured knobs** (blue, green, white, orange): each changes the value of the same colour on the screen. Drag up or down or scroll over a knob to turn it. Double-click resets it.
- **Volume knob** (small white knob, top left): the master output level.
- **Mode keys:** SYNTH, DRUM, TAPE and MIXER.
- **T1–T4:** pick the page inside a mode.
- **1–8:** sound slots. They choose a synth, or a kit in drum mode. With bank B selected, the slot light turns orange.
- **Transport:** REC, PLAY and STOP, plus the arrows for winding the tape.
- **Musical keys:** the keyboard along the bottom, with white and black keys.
- **Lights on the right edge:** the output level meter. The red light at the top means you are near the maximum level.
- **Speaker grille** (top left corner): decoration only; the sound comes from your computer.

All the controls sit in one block, separated by thin black lines, so each key reads as its own button. The black keys' row is case-coloured, with a black line around each black key. The WEB-1 name and the "by Snow Bayles - 2026" engraving run up the right edge of the case.

Everything on the device can be clicked or tapped, and almost everything also has a computer-keyboard shortcut.

## 3. Synth mode

Press **SYNTH** (Z). The T keys choose the page:

| Page | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **T1 ENGINE** | The engine's own four parameters (listed below) |
| **T2 ENVELOPE** | ATTACK · DECAY · SUSTAIN · RELEASE |
| **T3 FX** | The sound's own effect. Press T3 again to switch between ECHO, ROOM, CRUSH and SWIRL. Shift + T3 switches to the bank B effects (see below). |
| **T4 LFO** | RATE · AMOUNT · DEST (PITCH, AMP, PAN, TONE) · SHAPE (SINE, TRI, SQR, SAW) |

Each slot remembers its own engine settings, envelope, effect and LFO.

### Effects

Every synth slot and drum kit has one effect, chosen on the T3 page. There are 8 effects in two banks:

| Effect | Sound | Knobs |
|---|---|---|
| **ECHO** | Simple delay | TIME · REPEAT · TONE · MIX |
| **ROOM** | Room reverb | SIZE · DAMP · PRE · MIX |
| **CRUSH** | Bit crusher and drive | CRUSH · DRIVE · TONE · MIX |
| **SWIRL** | Phaser | RATE · DEPTH · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **BOUNCE** (⇧T3) | Ping-pong delay that bounces left and right, with tape-style wobble | TIME · WOBBLE · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **COIL** (⇧T3) | Spring reverb | TONE · TENSION · DAMP · MIX |
| **CUBE** (⇧T3) | Box-shaped room reverb; SHAPE goes from natural to metallic | SIZE · SHAPE · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **RADIO** (⇧T3) | Old radio or phone: narrow band, lower sample rate, crackle and dropouts | BAND · CRUSH · STATIC · MIX |

- **T3** opens the effect page. Press T3 again to step through ECHO, ROOM, CRUSH and SWIRL.
- **Shift + T3** (Shift + M) switches to bank B; the T3 light turns orange. Press Shift + T3 again to step through BOUNCE, COIL, CUBE and RADIO.
- Opening the effect page with T3 from another page keeps the sound's current effect, whichever bank it is in.
- Switching banks moves to the effect in the same position: ECHO ↔ BOUNCE, ROOM ↔ COIL, CRUSH ↔ CUBE, SWIRL ↔ RADIO.

### Bank A: keys 1–8

| Key | Engine | Character | Knobs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CLUSTER** | Detuned stack with a sub and a resonant filter | SPREAD · SUB · CUTOFF · RESO |
| 2 | **FM** | Bells, keys and metallic tones | RATIO · INDEX · STACK · DECAY |
| 3 | **STRING** | Plucked string | DAMP · BRIGHT · SUSTAIN · PICK |
| 4 | **PULSE** | Pulse-width wave, from thin to hollow | WIDTH · PWM · RATE · SUB |
| 5 | **ORGAN** | Drawbar organ | 16' · 8' · 4' · BRIGHT |
| 6 | **FOLD** | Wavefolder, from soft to gnarly | FOLD · BIAS · OCTAVE · TONE |
| 7 | **VOX** | Vowel and formant voice | VOWEL · FORMANT · BREATH · VIBRATO |
| 8 | **STORM** | Pitched noise and resonance | RESO · COLOR · TONE · SWEEP |

### Bank B: Shift + 1–8 (orange light)

| Keys | Engine | Character | Knobs |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⇧1 | **NEON** | Huge 7-voice supersaw, wide stereo, for synthwave leads and pads | DETUNE · MIX · WIDTH · CUTOFF |
| ⇧2 | **LADDER** | Fat mono bass and lead, with glide from the previous note | CUTOFF · EMPHASIS · CONTOUR · GLIDE |
| ⇧3 | **ORBIT** | Poly brass whose filter swells open on each note | SWELL · BITE · DETUNE · HPF |
| ⇧4 | **SYNC** | Hard-sync lead with a sweep on every note | SYNC · SWEEP · DECAY · METAL |
| ⇧5 | **WAVES** | Digital wavetable scan with optional bit crush | WAVE · SCAN · TIME · BITS |
| ⇧6 | **AURORA** | 80s string ensemble, wide and shimmering | ENSEMBLE · OCTAVE · BRIGHT · SPEED |
| ⇧7 | **ACID** | Squelchy bassline; overlap notes to make them slide | CUTOFF · RESO · ENV MOD · DECAY |
| ⇧8 | **HOOVER** | 90s rave lead that dives into each note | DIVE · DETUNE · CHORUS · TONE |

Tip: Shift + 1–8 works from any mode, and jumps straight to that synth.

## 4. Drum mode

Press **DRUM** (X), then pick a kit with **1–8**: BOX · DEEP · TINY · DIRT · TAPE · CLICK · AIR · ROOM.

Every musical key plays a different drum sound, and the screen flashes the pad you hit.

| Page | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **T1** | TUNE · DECAY · TONE · DRIVE |
| **T2 MIX** | KICK · SNARE · HATS · PERC levels |
| **T3 FX** | The kit's effect (press T3 again to change it, or Shift + T3 for bank B) |
| **T4 LFO** | Same as for synths |

## 5. Sampler

Any synth slot can hold a sample instead of its engine.

- **load sample:** pick an audio file. You can also drag an audio file onto the page.
- **record sample:** record from the microphone.
- **clear sample:** removes the sample and brings the slot's engine back.

The sample plays across the keys at different pitches. Its knobs are START · END · LOOP · TUNE.

## 6. Tape mode

Press **TAPE** (C). The tape has **4 tracks × 60 seconds**.

- **T1–T4** pick the track to record on. The selected track's number lights up in its colour.
- **REC** arms the tape and starts it. Whatever you play is recorded onto the selected track, on top of what is already there.
- **PLAY** plays the tape. **Shift + PLAY** plays it backwards.
- **STOP** stops it; press STOP twice to go back to the start.
- **← / →:** hold to wind the tape.

**Knobs:** JOG (move the tape head) · LOOP (loop length in bars) · REC LVL · SPEED.

SPEED works like real tape: record at one speed and play back at another, and the pitch changes.

**Looping:** **Q** sets a loop starting at the tape head, with the length set by the green knob.

**Editing:**

- **LIFT** (`[`) cuts the loop, or the current bar, out of the selected track.
- **DROP** (`]`) pastes it at the tape head.
- **Shift + LIFT** erases the whole track.

**Tape tricks:** in tape mode, hold one of these keys while the tape plays:

- **1:** BRAKE, slows the tape to a stop.
- **2:** STUTTER, repeats a tiny slice.
- **3:** REVERSE, plays backwards while held.

## 7. Mixer mode

Press **MIXER** (V). Each T page gives the four knobs a different job:

| Page | Blue | Green | White | Orange |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 LEVEL** | Track 1 | Track 2 | Track 3 | Track 4 |
| **T2 PAN** | Pan 1 | Pan 2 | Pan 3 | Pan 4 |
| **T3 EQ** | LOW | MID | HIGH | DRIVE (master) |
| **T4 MASTER** | OUT | COMP | INSTR (live-playing level) | – |

## 8. Sequencers

Press **=** to open the sequencer screen, then choose a type with T1, T2 or T3. **Shift + =** clears the sequence.

- **ENDLESS (T1):**
  - Hold Shift and tap notes to add steps.
  - Then hold any key to play the steps, transposed to that key.
  - Knobs: RATE · GATE · DIR (FWD, REV, PING, RND) · LATCH.
- **PATTERN (T2):**
  - A step grid. The blue knob picks a step; tap keys to switch notes on or off on that step.
  - The orange knob starts and stops the pattern.
  - Knobs: STEP · RATE · LENGTH · RUN.
- **ARP (T3):**
  - Hold a chord and it is played as an arpeggio.
  - Knobs: RATE · GATE · MODE (UP, DOWN, UPDN, RND) · RANGE (1–3 octaves).

Sequencers follow the tempo, and they record onto the tape like anything else you play.

## 9. FX rack

Press **I** for the master rack. It has four effects in a chain:

**DRIVE → CHORUS → DELAY → REVERB**

- T1–T4 select an effect. Press the same T again to switch it on or off.
- **Shift + I** switches every effect off.
- The four lights on the FX key show which effects are on.

| Effect | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **DRIVE** | DRIVE · TYPE (TAPE, TUBE, FUZZ) · TONE · MIX |
| **CHORUS** | RATE · DEPTH · WIDTH · MIX |
| **DELAY** | TIME (synced to tempo) · FEEDBK · AGE · MIX |
| **REVERB** | DECAY · TONE · SPRING (PLATE at zero) · MIX |

Delay and reverb tails ring out when you switch them off. The rack is included in the WAV export.

## 10. Tempo, count-in and quantize

Press **-** for the tempo screen.

| Knob | Normal | With Shift held |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | BPM (40–200) | SWING |
| Green | COUNT-IN (off, 1 bar, 2 bars) | BEATS per bar |
| White | QUANTIZE (off, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32) | SNAP on/off |
| Orange | Metronome CLICK level | – |

- **`** switches the metronome on or off.
- **Count-in:** pressing REC from stop clicks you in, and the tape starts on the downbeat. Press STOP or REC to cancel.
- **Quantize:** notes from the keys or MIDI snap to the grid, which follows the tape's bars. Notes that are only slightly late play straight away. Sequencers also start on the grid.
- **Snap:** loop, lift and drop line up with bars and beats.

## 11. MIDI

Click **connect midi** to use any connected MIDI keyboard. Supported:

- Note velocity
- Pitch bend
- Mod wheel, which sets the LFO amount
- Sustain pedal

Web MIDI needs Chrome or Edge.

## 12. Looks: dark mode, OLED and line art

The buttons under the device change how WEB-1 looks (on OS-1 they are in the **?** card):

- **dark mode:** a graphite case and keys with a paper-white screen. The black lines between the keys stay black. Switch it off for the light aluminium case with the black screen.
- **oled:** adds a soft glow (halation) to the screen, like light spreading on an OLED panel.
- **line art:** draws the whole device as flat outlines: thin lines for the keys, knob bases, keyboard and case edge, solid circles for the black keys, coloured knob caps and a white screen. Together with **dark mode** it becomes light lines on a dark-grey case with a black screen.

All three settings are remembered, and **reset all** does not change them.

## 13. Saving and exporting

- Everything saves automatically in your browser: sounds, slot settings, sequences, the mixer, the FX rack, samples and the tape.
- **export tape as wav** downloads the full tape mix, including the master FX.
- **reset all** erases every saved sound, sequence, sample and the tape. You are asked to confirm first.

Your work lives in the browser you made it in. Clearing that site's data, or using a private window, starts fresh. Export WAVs of anything you want to keep.

## 14. Your first song in 10 steps

1. Press **-** and set the BPM with the blue knob. Turn the green knob to **1 BAR** count-in, then press **-** again to close the screen.
2. Press **X** for drums and pick a kit with **1**. Press **C** for tape and **B** to select track 1.
3. Press **R**. After the count-in, play a beat on the keys for a few bars, then press **Enter**.
4. Press **Enter** twice to rewind, then press **Q** to loop the first bars.
5. Press **N** to select track 2. Press **Shift + 7** for ACID and play a bassline while recording with **R**. Overlap notes to make them slide.
6. Select track 3 with **M**. Press **Shift + 6** for AURORA and record some long chords.
7. Select track 4 with **,**. Press **Shift + 1** for NEON and record a lead.
8. Press **V** for the mixer and balance the four tracks with the knobs.
9. Press **I**, then switch on DELAY (T3) and REVERB (T4) for space.
10. Click **export tape as wav** to download your song.

## 15. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No sound | Click the device or press a key first, since browsers need a click before audio can play. Check the volume knob and the MIXER levels. |
| Keys don't play | Click the page so it has keyboard focus, and make sure no text field is selected. |
| Recording is silent | Check REC LVL (tape mode) and that the right track is selected. With count-in on, recording starts after the clicks. |
| Microphone or MIDI doesn't work | Serve the page from `localhost` or HTTPS, and use Chrome or Edge for MIDI. |
| Crackles | Close other heavy tabs. Very big chords on NEON or HOOVER use the most CPU. |
| I want a fresh start | Click **reset all**. |
