## 1. The device at a glance

- **Screen** (top left, set flush into the case with no frame): shows the current mode and an animated picture of the sound. It fills its whole tile: the title runs along the top edge, the picture sits in the middle, and the four coloured values along the bottom edge belong to the four knobs. It is always a black display, whichever finish you choose.
- **Four coloured knobs** (blue, green, white, orange), each sitting in a ring with a short pointer near its edge: each changes the value of the same colour on the screen. Drag up or down or scroll over a knob to turn it. Double-click resets it.
- **Volume knob** (small white knob, top left): the master output level.
- **Metronome key** (below the volume knob): opens the tempo screen (the **-** key).
- **Mode keys:** SYNTH, DRUM, TAPE and MIXER. SYNTH lights orange while the synths & presets menu is open (Shift + SYNTH), DRUM while the drum machine is open (Shift + DRUM).
- **T1–T4:** pick the page inside a mode.
- **1–8:** sound slots. They choose a synth, or a kit in drum mode. With bank B selected, the slot light turns orange. Keys 1–3 are labelled with the tape tricks, and key 4 with SAMPLE, the drum-mode sample editor (Shift + 4). All eight numbers sit at the same height in their rings, labelled or not.
- **Transport:** REC (a red disc inside the key's ring, Shift + REC for advanced recording), PLAY and STOP, plus the ← → arrows: pitch bend in synth mode, tape winding in the other modes.
- **Musical keys:** the keyboard along the bottom, with white and black keys.
- **Lights on the right edge:** the output level meter. The red light at the top means you are near the maximum level.
- **Speaker grille** (top left corner, a round grille of dots): decoration only; the sound comes from your computer.
- **FX key:** its four dots show which master effects are on, in either bank; it lights orange while FX bank B is on screen.

All the controls sit in one block, separated by thin black lines, so each key reads as its own button. Each key has a large ring with a bold icon or number. In the black keys' row, the outer key of each group widens out to the next white-key line, so the row has no gaps, while each round black cap stays centred over its note. The row sits on the same black plate as everything else, so the rounded corners of the black keys meet clean black lines with no light specks. The WEB-1 name and the "by Snow Bayles - 2026" engraving run up the right edge of the case.

Everything on the device can be clicked or tapped, and almost everything also has a computer-keyboard shortcut. The controls card opens from the **guide** button under the device, or with **/**.

## 3. Synth mode

Press **SYNTH** (Z). The T keys choose the page:

| Page | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **T1 ENGINE** | The engine's own four parameters (listed below) |
| **T2 ENVELOPE** | ATTACK · DECAY · SUSTAIN · RELEASE |
| **T3 FX** | The sound's own effect. Press T3 again to switch between ECHO, ROOM, CRUSH and SWIRL. Shift + T3 switches to the bank B effects (see below). |
| **T4 LFO** | RATE · AMOUNT · DEST (PITCH, AMP, PAN, TONE) · SHAPE (SINE, TRI, SQR, SAW) |

Each slot remembers its own engine settings, envelope, effect and LFO.

### Effects

Every synth slot and drum kit has one effect, chosen on the T3 page. There are 8 effects in two banks:

| Effect | Sound | Knobs |
|---|---|---|
| **ECHO** | Simple delay | TIME · REPEAT · TONE · MIX |
| **ROOM** | Room reverb | SIZE · DAMP · PRE · MIX |
| **CRUSH** | Bit crusher and drive | CRUSH · DRIVE · TONE · MIX |
| **SWIRL** | Phaser | RATE · DEPTH · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **BOUNCE** (⇧T3) | Ping-pong delay that bounces left and right, with tape-style wobble | TIME · WOBBLE · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **COIL** (⇧T3) | Spring reverb | TONE · TENSION · DAMP · MIX |
| **CUBE** (⇧T3) | Box-shaped room reverb; SHAPE goes from natural to metallic | SIZE · SHAPE · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **RADIO** (⇧T3) | Old radio or phone: narrow band, lower sample rate, crackle and dropouts | BAND · CRUSH · STATIC · MIX |

- **T3** opens the effect page. Press T3 again to step through ECHO, ROOM, CRUSH and SWIRL.
- **Shift + T3** (Shift + M) switches to bank B; the T3 light turns orange. Press Shift + T3 again to step through BOUNCE, COIL, CUBE and RADIO.
- Opening the effect page with T3 from another page keeps the sound's current effect, whichever bank it is in.
- Every effect screen shows its picture on the left and its two main values as big numbers on the right, in the colours of their knobs.
- Switching banks moves to the effect in the same position: ECHO ↔ BOUNCE, ROOM ↔ COIL, CRUSH ↔ CUBE, SWIRL ↔ RADIO.

### Synths & presets menu: Shift + SYNTH

Hold **Shift** and press **SYNTH** (Shift + Z) to open a menu of every synth with its presets. The SYNTH key lights orange while it is open.

- **Blue knob:** scroll through all 16 synths, bank A and bank B. The synth loads as soon as it is highlighted.
- **Green knob:** scroll through that synth's presets. Each one loads as you turn, so play the keys while you browse. **INIT** brings back the synth's default sound.
- Each synth has INIT plus 4 presets, for example E.PIANO, BELL, SLAP BASS and METAL on FM, or FANFARE, MELLOW, SFORZANDO and SECTION on BRASS.
- A preset sets the synth's four knobs, its envelope and its effect. You can tweak any of them afterwards; your changes are saved as usual.
- Your own presets appear at the end of each synth's list, marked with a dot.
- Press **Shift + SYNTH** again, or any T key, to close the menu.

### Saving your own presets: Shift + T1

In synth mode, hold **Shift** and press **T1** (Shift + B) to open **SAVE PRESET** for the synth you are on. It works from the synths & presets menu too. T1 lights orange while it is open. The preset is saved on that synth, in its bank, with its four knobs, envelope, effect and LFO.

| Knob | Job |
|---|---|
| **Blue: LETTER** | Picks the letter at the cursor: A–Z, 0–9, - or a space |
| **Green: CURSOR** | Moves along the name (10 characters) |
| **White: SAVE** | Turn it all the way right (from NO to YES) to save. A preset with the same name is overwritten |
| **Orange: DELETE** | When the name matches one of your presets, turn it all the way right to delete that preset |

On a computer you can also type the name on the keyboard (Backspace and ← → work too). **Enter** saves and closes, **Esc** closes without saving. While the screen is open the letter keys type instead of playing. If the current sound is one of your presets, its name is already filled in, so you can overwrite or delete it straight away. Each synth holds up to 16 of your presets.

Bank B is reached from synth mode only: press **SYNTH** (Z) first, then Shift + 1–8. In drum mode Shift + 4 opens the pad sample editor instead, and in the other modes Shift + 1–8 does nothing.

## 4. Drum mode

Press **DRUM** (X), then pick a kit with **1–8**: BOX · DEEP · TINY · DIRT · TAPE · CLICK · AIR · ROOM.

Every musical key plays a different drum sound, and the screen flashes the pad you hit.

| Page | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **T1** | TUNE · DECAY · TONE · DRIVE |
| **T2 MIX** | KICK · SNARE · HATS · PERC levels |
| **T3 FX** | The kit's effect (press T3 again to change it, or Shift + T3 for bank B) |
| **T4 LFO** | Same as for synths |

### Pad samples

Each kit can have your own sample on any pad, so you could put kick.wav on KICK and keep the built-in sounds everywhere else.

1. In drum mode, pick a kit and hit the pad you want to replace. The screen rings the selected pad.
2. Click **load sample** (or drag an audio file onto the page), or **record sample** to record one from the microphone.
3. The sample now plays on that pad only, in that kit. A small square on the pad and the file name at the top of the screen show it.

The kit's TUNE changes its pitch and TONE how bright it is. How long it plays is set in the pad sample editor (Shift + 4), with START, END and the play mode. On the upper row of keys it plays higher, like the built-in sounds. **clear sample** puts the built-in sound back on the selected pad. Pad samples are saved, and **reset all** erases them.

#### Editing a pad sample: Shift + 4

Key 4 is labelled **SAMPLE** in orange. In drum mode, hold **Shift** and press **4** to open the sample editor for the selected pad, as you would edit a sample in synth mode. Key 4 lights orange while it is open, and the screen shows the sample's waveform with the part that plays in white. While the pad plays, an orange line runs through the waveform, one for each hit that is still sounding. The play mode is shown in the top corner, fades are drawn as orange ramps, and a green line under the waveform marks a loop.

| Knob | What it does | Shift + knob | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blue: START** | Where the sample starts playing | **PLAY** | **ONE SHOT** plays the whole part from one hit · **GATE** plays only while the key is held · **LOOP** loops the part while the key is held |
| **Green: END** | Where it stops | **FADE IN** | Fades the start in, up to 2 s |
| **White: TUNE** | Pitch, ±12 semitones | **FADE OUT** | Fades the end out, up to 2 s (ONE SHOT and GATE) |
| **Orange: REVERSE** | Plays the sample backwards | **LEVEL** | The pad's volume |

The kit's TUNE, TONE and level still shape a sampled pad. Its length now comes from START, END and the play mode, so the kit's DECAY knob no longer cuts samples short. When a sequencer plays a GATE or LOOP pad, the pad stops at the end of each step.

Hit another pad to edit that pad's sample; pads without a sample show a reminder to load one. Loading a sample while the editor is open puts it on the selected pad. Press **Shift + 4** again, or any T key, to close the editor. The settings are saved with the pad, and loading a new sample on a pad resets them.

### Drum machine

Press **Shift + DRUM** (Shift + X) to open the drum machine. The DRUM light turns orange. It has 12 pads (KICK to SHAKE), each built from **two synth voices and a filter**, plus a **16-step pattern**. Keys play the pads and select the one you edit. The left of the screen always shows the pad grid, the pad's name and a live waveform.

| Page | Knobs | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| **T1 VOICE 1** | TUNE · WAVE (sine, triangle, square, noise) · DECAY · BEND (pitch drop) | Both voices side by side, with a tune dial, wave shape and decay and pitch curves |
| **T2 VOICE 2** | Same, for the second voice | Same, with voice 2 highlighted |
| **T3 FILTER** | MODE (off, low, high, band, notch, peak, ring, crush) · CUTOFF · RESO · MIX (voice 1 ↔ voice 2) | Mode dial and the filter's response curve |
| **T4 STEPS** | PAD · LENGTH (1–16 steps) · LEVEL · RUN | A 16-step grid for all 12 pads, with the playhead |

- On **STEPS**, the first 16 keys switch steps on and off for the selected pad. Hold Shift and press a key to pick a different pad.
- The pattern follows the tempo and swing. It keeps playing after you close the drum machine, and it records onto the tape like anything else.
- Press **Shift + DRUM** again to close it. Your pads and pattern are saved.

## 5. Sampler

Any synth slot can hold a sample instead of its engine.

- **load sample:** pick an audio file. You can also drag an audio file onto the page.
- **record sample:** record from the microphone.
- **clear sample:** removes the sample and brings the slot's engine back.

The sample plays across the keys at different pitches. While it plays, an orange line runs through the waveform, one for each note that sounds.

| Knob | Job | Shift + knob | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blue: START** | Where it starts | **PLAY** | **ONE SHOT** plays the whole part from one tap · **GATE** plays only while the key is held · **LOOP** loops the part while the key is held |
| **Green: END** | Where it ends | **FADE IN** | Fades the start in, up to 2 s |
| **White: TUNE** | ±12 semitones | **FADE OUT** | Fades the end out, up to 2 s (ONE SHOT and GATE) |
| **Orange: REVERSE** | Plays backwards | **LEVEL** | Volume |

The screen and knobs work exactly like the drum pad editor (Shift + 4 in drum mode): the slot and the sample's length at the top left, the play mode and a light that blinks on each note at the top right, fades as orange ramps, a green line under the waveform for a loop, and the waveform flipped when REVERSE is on, so the part that plays always reads from left to right.

## 6. Tape mode

Press **TAPE** (C). The tape has **4 tracks × 60 seconds**.

- **T1–T4** pick the track to record on. The selected track's number is shown large in the top-left corner, in its colour.
- **REC** arms the tape and starts it. Whatever you play is recorded onto the selected track, on top of what is already there.
- **PLAY** plays the tape. **Shift + PLAY** plays it backwards.
- **STOP** stops it; press STOP twice to go back to the start.
- **← / →:** hold to wind the tape.

**Knobs:** JOG (move the tape head) · LOOP (loop length in bars) · REC LVL · SPEED.

SPEED works like real tape: record at one speed and play back at another, and the pitch changes.

**The tape screen:** the time runs along the top with the bar and beat under it. Two big reels turn as the tape moves, and the tape itself runs from reel to reel over two rollers and the head, its marks sliding along as it plays: the left reel empties and the right one fills as you go. The big number in the top-left corner is the selected track, in its colour, and the **FX** box under it lights orange while any master effect is on. The orange line on the right edge shows the SPEED. The four tracks run along the bottom as slim lanes with bar and beat lines, and the red line is the tape head.

### Advanced REC: Shift + REC

Hold **Shift** and press **REC** (Shift + R) to open **ADVANCED REC**. The REC light turns orange. The screen shows the REC disc and the selected track on the left (READY, RECORDING, or blinking while it waits), and one panel per knob.

| Knob | Job | Shift + knob | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blue: SOURCE** | What is recorded: INSTRUMENT (what you play), MIC, or BOTH. The panel shows the input level | **THRESHOLD** | How loud the input must get to start an ON SOUND recording (the white line on the level meter) |
| **Green: KEEP OLD** | What happens to what is already on the track where you record: 100% overdubs (the default), 0% replaces it, in between fades it a little more with each pass | **MONITOR** | How loud you hear the microphone while you record |
| **White: START** | KEY starts as soon as you press REC · ON SOUND rolls the tape and starts recording when the input passes the threshold · NEXT BAR rolls the tape and starts on the next bar line | **PUNCH** | LOOP AREA records only between the loop markers, even with LOOP off |
| **Orange: AUTO STOP** | OFF, or stop recording after 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars; the tape keeps playing | **COUNT-IN** | The same count-in as on the tempo screen |

Press **REC** again while it waits to cancel, or **STOP**. The settings are saved, and any that are not the usual ones are listed in small text at the top right of the tape screen, for example REPLACE, MIC or STOP 2 BAR. The microphone needs your permission and works on `localhost` or HTTPS.

### Erasing the whole tape: Shift + TAPE

Hold **Shift** and press **TAPE** (Shift + C) to open **ERASE TAPE?**. The TAPE key lights orange. To go ahead, press **T1, T2, T3 and T4**: each box lights in its track colour as you press it, and when the fourth is pressed the tape is wiped, with a progress bar. There is no undo. Until you press the fourth key, **Shift + TAPE** again or any mode key cancels. Musical keys don't play while the prompt is open.

### System menu: Shift + 8

In tape mode, hold **Shift** and press **8** (labelled **MENU**) to open the system menu. Key 8 lights orange. It is laid out like the synths & presets menu: a picture of the section on the left, the sections in blue, the items and their values in green.

| Knob | Job |
|---|---|
| **Blue: SECTION** | BATTERY · TIME · TIMEZONE · STORAGE · DISPLAY · ABOUT |
| **Green: ITEM** | The setting inside the section |
| **White: SET / CONFIRM / OPEN** | Changes the setting; for an erase, turn it all the way to YES to confirm |

| Section | Items |
|---|---|
| **BATTERY** | Level, charging or on battery, time left (shown where the browser reports a battery) |
| **TIME** | Hour, minute, 12 or 24 hour clock, date, and USE DEVICE TIME to go back to the real clock |
| **TIMEZONE** | AUTO (this device) or a city from UTC−10 to UTC+12, with the local time |
| **STORAGE** | Space used, number of samples and user presets, ERASE SAMPLES, ERASE PRESETS, ERASE TAPE (opens the erase prompt) and ERASE EVERYTHING |
| **DISPLAY** | Brightness (20–100%), screen sleep after 1, 5, 10 or 30 minutes (any key or knob wakes it), and a clock in the title bar |
| **ABOUT** | Device, version, engines, tape size and MIDI status |

The settings are saved. The menu is built for the hardware version to come: in the browser, battery and storage show what the browser can see, and the timezones are fixed offsets without summer time.

**Looping:** **Q** sets a loop starting at the tape head, with the length set by the green knob.

**Editing:**

- **LIFT** (`[`) cuts the loop, or the current bar, out of the selected track.
- **DROP** (`]`) pastes it at the tape head.
- **Shift + LIFT** erases the whole track.

**More edits: Shift + T1–T4** work on the selected track, over the loop if LOOP is on, otherwise over the bar at the tape head. While you hold Shift, the tape screen shows the four edits and outlines the bar they will use.

Thin vertical lines on the tracks mark every bar (every fourth bar is brighter), with fainter lines for the beats, so you can see where to cut and loop.

| Keys | Edit | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Shift + T1** | COPY | Copies the part without cutting it. Press **DROP** to paste it at the tape head. |
| **Shift + T2** | CROP | Keeps only that part of the track and erases everything else. |
| **Shift + T3** | DOUBLE | Pastes the part again right after itself, to repeat a bar or a loop. |
| **Shift + T4** | ERASE | Clears only that part of the track. |

**Tape tricks:** in tape mode, hold one of these keys while the tape plays:

- **1:** BRAKE, slows the tape to a stop.
- **2:** STUTTER, repeats a tiny slice.
- **3:** REVERSE, plays backwards while held.

## 7. Mixer mode

Press **MIXER** (V). Each T page gives the four knobs a different job, and holding **Shift** while you turn a knob reaches a second set of settings on every page. The title bar shows what Shift does on the page.

| Page | Knobs | Shift + knobs |
|---|---|---|
| **T1 LEVEL & PAN** | Track 1–4 level | Track 1–4 pan, left or right |
| **T2 MULTIBAND COMP** | LOW · MID · HIGH compression amount · MAKEUP gain | LOW X and HIGH X crossover frequencies · ATTACK · RELEASE |
| **T3 EQ** | Gain of the 4 bands: LOW · LO MID · HI MID · HIGH (±12 dB) | Slide each band's frequency |
| **T4 MASTER** | OUT · COMP · INSTR (live-playing level) · LIMIT | CEILING · LIM REL (limiter release) · COMP SPD · WIDTH (0–200%) |

- **T1** shows four channel strips: a fader and level meter for each track, with its pan underneath.
- **T2** splits the sound into three bands. The screen shows each band's level, its threshold line and how much it is being turned down (in orange). With LOW, MID and HIGH at 0 the compressor is off and the sound passes untouched.
- **T3** draws the real EQ curve; each coloured dot is a band, and holding Shift shows rings around the dots while you slide them.
- **T4**: LIMIT pushes the mix into a limiter that never goes above the CEILING, so the mix gets louder without clipping. At 0 it is off. WIDTH narrows the stereo image (0% is mono) or widens it (up to 200%). The screen shows the output meter with the ceiling, and how much the compressor and limiter are turning the sound down.

## 8. Sequencers

Press **=** to open the sequencer screen, then choose a type with T1, T2, T3 or T4. **Shift + =** clears the sequence.

- **ENDLESS (T1):**
  - Hold Shift and tap notes to add steps.
  - Then hold any key to play the steps, transposed to that key.
  - Knobs: RATE · GATE · DIR (FWD, REV, PING, RND) · LATCH.
- **PATTERN (T2):**
  - A step grid. The blue knob picks a step; tap keys to switch notes on or off on that step.
  - The orange knob starts and stops the pattern.
  - Knobs: STEP · RATE · LENGTH · RUN.
- **ARP (T3):**
  - Hold a chord and it is played as an arpeggio.
  - Knobs: RATE · GATE · MODE (UP, DOWN, UPDN, RND) · RANGE (1–3 octaves).
- **CHAIN (T4):** an endless step sequencer you write note by note, with chords, long notes and rests.
  - **Add notes:** hold **Shift** and press keys. Keys pressed together become one chord step. Keep Shift held until you have added all the notes you want.
  - **Long notes:** add a note with Shift, keep the key held and press **→**; each press makes that step one step longer.
  - **Rest:** **Shift + →** adds an empty step.
  - **Undo:** **Shift + ←** removes the last step.
  - **Play:** press any key and the chain plays transposed to it; the low C on the keyboard plays it as written. With HOLD on it keeps playing after you let go.
  - The screen shows the number of steps, the rate and gate, and the chain itself: chord notes stacked in each step, long steps wider (underlined in green) and rests dashed.
  - Knobs: RATE · GATE · OCTAVE (±2) · HOLD.

Sequencers follow the tempo, and they record onto the tape like anything else you play.

## 9. FX rack

Press **I** for the master rack. It has four effects in a chain:

**DRIVE → CHORUS → DELAY → REVERB**

- T1–T4 select an effect. Press the same T again to switch it on or off.
- **Shift + FX** (Shift + I) switches to **bank B**, a second chain of four effects; the FX key lights orange while bank B is on screen. Shift + FX again goes back to bank A. Effects in both banks can be on at the same time.
- The four lights on the FX key show which effects are on, in either bank.

| Effect | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **DRIVE** | DRIVE · TYPE (TAPE, TUBE, FUZZ) · TONE · MIX |
| **CHORUS** | RATE · DEPTH · WIDTH · MIX |
| **DELAY** | TIME (synced to tempo) · FEEDBK · AGE · MIX |
| **REVERB** | DECAY · TONE · SPRING (PLATE at zero) · MIX |

**Bank B** (Shift + FX), after bank A in the chain: **FILTER → GATE → FLANGER → LO-FI**

| Effect | Knobs |
|---|---|
| **FILTER** | FREQ (turn left for low-pass, right for high-pass, centre is open) · RESO · WOBBLE (sweeps with the beat) · MIX |
| **GATE** | RATE (1/4 to 1/32, synced to tempo) · DEPTH · SMOOTH · MIX |
| **FLANGER** | RATE · DEPTH · FEEDBK · MIX |
| **LO-FI** | CRUSH (12 down to 3 bits, with hiss) · TONE · WOW · MIX |

Delay and reverb tails ring out when you switch them off. Both banks are included in the WAV export.

## 10. Tempo, count-in and quantize

Press **-** for the tempo screen.

| Knob | Normal | With Shift held |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | BPM (40–200) | SWING |
| Green | COUNT-IN (off, 1 bar, 2 bars) | BEATS per bar |
| White | QUANTIZE (off, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32) | SNAP on/off |
| Orange | Metronome CLICK level | – |

- **`** switches the metronome on or off.
- **Count-in:** pressing REC from stop clicks you in, and the tape starts on the downbeat. Press STOP or REC to cancel.
- **Quantize:** notes from the keys or MIDI snap to the grid, which follows the tape's bars. Notes that are only slightly late play straight away. Sequencers also start on the grid.
- **Snap:** loop, lift and drop line up with bars and beats.

## 11. MIDI

Click **connect midi** to use any connected MIDI keyboard. Supported:

- Note velocity
- Pitch bend
- Mod wheel, which sets the LFO amount
- Sustain pedal

Web MIDI needs Chrome or Edge.

## 12. Looks: dark mode, OLED and line art

The buttons under the device change how WEB-1 looks (on OS-1 they are in the **?** card):

- **dark mode:** a graphite case and keys, with the same black screen. The black lines between the keys stay black. Switch it off for the light aluminium case with the black screen.
- **oled:** adds a subtle glow (halation) to the screen, like light spreading on an OLED panel.
- **line art:** draws the whole device as flat outlines: thin lines for the keys, knob bases, keyboard and case edge, solid circles for the black keys, coloured knob caps and a black screen. Together with **dark mode** it becomes light lines on a dark-grey case, still with a black screen.

All three settings are remembered, and **reset all** does not change them.

### The screens

Every picture is drawn to fill the screen, from edge to edge between the title and the four values.

- **Bank A (A1–A8):** CLUSTER, FM (a round planet with a moon on a circular orbit), PULSE, ORGAN (four tall drawbars), FOLD and STORM each draw their sound large across the screen.
- **STRINGS (A3):** a vintage string-machine panel in flat lines. Its four sliders (TONE, PHASE, CELLO, VIB) follow the four knobs in their colours, the PHASER light pulses at the phaser's speed, and the level meter and two-octave keyboard light up as you play.
- **HERO (A7):** an arcade space shooter. A pixel ship follows your pitch and glides with GLIDE, every note fires a laser, and aliens burst into pixels with a running score.
- **VELVET (B4):** a retro android in thin flat lines. Its LED visor lights up like an equaliser for your chord, its ear discs and the two speaker stacks pump with the level.
- **BRASS (B5):** a vintage polysynth in isometric line art. Its keys go down in orange as you play, its four panel knobs follow the four encoders, and a raised display traces the filter envelope, with a needle VU meter and A·D·S·R faders beside it.
- **NEON (B1):** a synthwave sunset in flat colour bands: a striped sun over a perspective grid that scrolls faster as you play.
- **AURORA (B6):** flat aurora curtains rippling at the ensemble speed, over stars and layered hills.
- **HOOVER (B8):** three thick saw waves in blue, green and white swirling over each other. At each note they bend up from a lower pitch (DIVE, also shown as a small bar), DETUNE pulls them apart, CHORUS makes them sway and the orange bar shows TONE.
- **LADDER, ORBIT, ACID:** live filter curve, ringed planet and resonant peak.
- **Sampler and pad sample editor:** both use the same screen: the waveform with START and END markers, fade ramps, a green loop mark, the play mode, and an orange playhead for each note or hit that is still sounding.
- **Effects (T3):** all eight effects draw their picture on the left with two big numbers on the right.
- **Tape:** big turning reels with the tape running between them, the time at the top, the track number large in its colour with an FX light, the speed on the right edge, and four slim tracks with bar and beat lines. Hold Shift to see the four edits (COPY · CROP · DOUBLE · ERASE) over T1–T4 and an outline around the part they will change; the name of the edit you just used flashes on screen.
- **Mixer:** channel strips with thick meters and pans below (T1), three compressor bands (T2), the real EQ curve with four dots (T3), and output and gain-reduction meters (T4).

## 13. Saving and exporting

- Everything saves automatically in your browser: sounds, slot settings, sequences, the mixer, the FX rack, samples and the tape.
- **export tape as wav** downloads the full tape mix, including the master FX.
- **reset all** erases every saved sound, sequence, sample and the tape. You are asked to confirm first.
- The **system menu** (Shift + 8 in tape mode) can erase just the samples, just your presets, just the tape, or everything, and shows how much space is used.

Your work lives in the browser you made it in. Clearing that site's data, or using a private window, starts fresh. Export WAVs of anything you want to keep.

## 14. Your first song in 10 steps

1. Press **-** and set the BPM with the blue knob. Turn the green knob to **1 BAR** count-in, then press **-** again to close the screen.
2. Press **X** for drums and pick a kit with **1**. Press **C** for tape and **B** to select track 1.
3. Press **R**. After the count-in, play a beat on the keys for a few bars, then press **Enter**.
4. Press **Enter** twice to rewind, then press **Q** to loop the first bars.
5. Press **N** to select track 2. Press **Z** for synth, then **Shift + 7** for ACID and play a bassline while recording with **R**. Overlap notes to make them slide.
6. Press **C** and select track 3 with **M**. Press **Z**, then **Shift + 6** for AURORA and record some long chords. If you shaped a sound you like, press **Shift + B** to save it as a preset, type a name and press **Enter**.
7. Press **C** and select track 4 with **,**. Press **Z**, then **Shift + 1** for NEON and record a lead.
8. Press **V** for the mixer and balance the four tracks with the knobs.
9. Press **I**, then switch on DELAY (T3) and REVERB (T4) for space.
10. Click **export tape as wav** to download your song.

## 15. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No sound | Click the device or press a key first, since browsers need a click before audio can play. Check the volume knob and the MIXER levels. |
| Keys don't play | Click the page so it has keyboard focus, and make sure no text field is selected. |
| Recording is silent | Check REC LVL (tape mode) and that the right track is selected. With count-in on, recording starts after the clicks. |
| The screen goes black after a while | SCREEN SLEEP is on in the system menu (Shift + 8 in tape mode, DISPLAY). Any key or knob wakes it. |
| The screen looks dim | Turn BRIGHTNESS up in the system menu (DISPLAY). |
| REC blinks but nothing records | ADVANCED REC (Shift + REC) is set to start ON SOUND or on the NEXT BAR. Play louder or lower the THRESHOLD, wait for the bar, or set START back to KEY. |
| Recording wipes what was already on the track | KEEP OLD in ADVANCED REC is below 100%. Turn it back up to overdub. |
| Recording stops by itself | AUTO STOP is on in ADVANCED REC. Set it to OFF. |
| Microphone or MIDI doesn't work | Serve the page from `localhost` or HTTPS, and use Chrome or Edge for MIDI. |
| The letter keys type instead of playing notes | The SAVE PRESET screen is open. Press **Esc** to close it, or **Enter** to save. |
| Crackles | Close other heavy tabs. Very big chords on NEON or HOOVER use the most CPU. |
| I want a fresh start | Click **reset all**. |
