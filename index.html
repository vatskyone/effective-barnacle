OS-1 · CONTROLS

KnobsDrag up or down on the four coloured knobs. Each matches the same-coloured value on the screen. Double-tap resets. The small white knob is the volume.
ModesThe first four keys under the screen: SYNTH · DRUM · TAPE · MIXER. FX is at the end of that row, SEQ (green steps) in the row below.
T1 – T4In synth/drum: ENGINE · ENVELOPE · FX · LFO (tap T3 again to change effect). In tape: select track 1–4. In mixer: LEVEL · PAN · EQ · MASTER.
1 – 8Sound slot: 8 synth engines, or 8 drum kits in drum mode. Hold SHIFT and tap 1–8 for synth bank B (orange light).
KeysTwo octaves, low to high from left to right. Hold SHIFT and tap ← or → to change octave.
● ▶ ■REC · PLAY · STOP. REC starts the tape and overdubs what you play onto the selected track. STOP twice rewinds. SHIFT + PLAY plays in reverse.
← →Hold to wind the tape.
LoopThe orange loop key switches LOOP on/off from the current position (length = green knob in tape mode). ↑ LIFT cuts the loop or bar from the track, ↓ DROP pastes it at the tape head. SHIFT + LIFT erases the track.
Tape tricksIn tape mode hold 1 = brake, 2 = stutter, 3 = reverse.
SEQSequencers. With the screen open, T1/T2/T3 pick the type. ENDLESS: hold SHIFT and tap notes to add steps, then hold any key to play them transposed. PATTERN: blue knob picks a step, tap keys to toggle notes on it, orange knob starts/stops. ARP: hold a chord. SHIFT + SEQ clears.
FXMaster rack: DRIVE → CHORUS → DELAY → REVERB. T1–T4 pick an effect, tap the same T again to switch it on/off. SHIFT + FX switches all off.
TempoThe metronome-shaped key opens TEMPO: count-in (green), quantize (white); hold SHIFT for swing, beats and snap. The clock key switches the metronome on/off.
Sampler“load sample” / “record sample” above puts a sound into the current synth slot and plays it across the keys. “clear sample” brings the engine back.
SavingSounds, sequences, samples and the tape are saved on this phone automatically. “export tape as wav” shares the mix.
No sound?Turn the volume up and switch the phone's silent mode off.
Full screenHold the phone sideways. In Safari tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”: OS-1 then opens like an app and fills the screen.

Tap outside the buttons to close.

IFX RACK: four master effects in a chain, DRIVE → CHORUS → DELAY → REVERB. T1–T4 pick an effect, press the same T again to switch it on/off. Shift+FX switches all off. The four lights on the FX key show which are on. Delay is synced to the tempo; delay and reverb tails ring out when switched off. The rack is included in the WAV export.

Count-inOn the TEMPO screen, green knob = COUNT-IN (off / 1 bar / 2 bars). Pressing REC from stop then clicks you in and the tape starts exactly on the downbeat. Press STOP or REC again to cancel.
QuantizeOn the TEMPO screen, white knob = QUANTIZE (off, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32). Notes you play on the keys or over MIDI snap to that grid, which follows the tape's bars; notes only a hair late play straight away. Sequencers start on the grid too. Hold SHIFT on the TEMPO screen for SWING, BEATS and SNAP (loop, lift and drop line up with bars and beats).

Tape: 4 tracks × 60 seconds, variable speed (orange knob in tape mode) — recording at one speed and playing at another shifts pitch, like real tape.
