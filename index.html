EncodersDrag up/down or scroll over the four coloured knobs. Each matches the same-coloured value on the screen. Double-click resets.
Z X C VModes: SYNTH · DRUM · TAPE · MIXER
B N M ,T1–T4. In synth/drum: ENGINE · ENVELOPE · FX · LFO (press T3 again to change effect). In tape: select track 1–4. In mixer: LEVEL · PAN · EQ · MASTER.
1 – 8Sound slot: 8 synth engines, or 8 drum kits in drum mode.
A…' rowMusical keys (W E T Y U O P are the black keys). Shift+← / → changes octave.
R · Space · EnterREC · PLAY · STOP. REC starts the tape and overdubs what you play onto the selected track. STOP twice rewinds. Shift+PLAY plays in reverse.
← →Hold to wind the tape.
QLOOP on/off from the current position (length = green knob in tape mode).
[ ]LIFT (cut loop/bar from the track) · DROP (paste it at the tape head). Shift+LIFT erases the track.
= SEQ: sequencers. With the screen open, T1/T2/T3 pick the type. ENDLESS: hold Shift and tap notes to add steps, then hold any key to play them transposed. PATTERN: blue knob picks a step, tap keys to toggle notes on it, orange knob starts/stops. ARP: hold a chord. Shift+SEQ clears.
Tape tricksIn tape mode hold 1 = brake, 2 = stutter, 3 = reverse.
Sampler“load sample” / “record sample” (or drop an audio file on the page) puts a sound into the current synth slot and plays it across the keys. “clear sample” brings the engine back.
MIDI“connect midi” enables any attached keyboard: velocity, pitch bend, mod wheel (LFO amount), sustain pedal.
SavingSounds, sequences, samples and the tape are saved in this browser automatically. “export tape as wav” downloads the mix.
