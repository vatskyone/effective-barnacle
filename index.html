OS-1 · CONTROLS

KnobsDrag up or down on the four coloured knobs. Each matches the same-coloured value on the screen. Double-tap resets. The small white knob is the volume.
ModesThe first four keys under the screen: SYNTH · DRUM · TAPE · MIXER. FX is at the end of that row, SEQ (green steps) in the row below.
T1 – T4In synth/drum: ENGINE · ENVELOPE · FX · LFO (tap T3 again to change effect). In tape: select track 1–4. In mixer: LEVEL & PAN · MULTIBAND COMP · EQ · MASTER (hold SHIFT on any mixer page for its extra knobs).
1 – 8Sound slot: 8 synth engines, or 8 drum kits in drum mode. In synth mode, hold SHIFT and tap 1–8 for synth bank B (orange light). In drum mode, SHIFT + 4 (SAMPLE) edits the selected pad's sample.
Shift + SYNTHSYNTHS & PRESETS menu : every synth in one list (blue knob) and its presets (green knob); both load as you turn, and the keys still play so you can try them. Shift + SYNTH again or any T key closes it.
KeysTwo octaves, low to high from left to right. Hold SHIFT and tap ← or → to change octave.
● ▶ ■REC · PLAY · STOP. REC starts the tape and overdubs what you play onto the selected track. STOP twice rewinds. SHIFT + PLAY plays in reverse.
← →In synth mode: hold for pitch bend down / up. In the other modes: hold to wind the tape.
LoopThe orange loop key switches LOOP on/off from the current position (length = green knob in tape mode). ↑ LIFT cuts the loop or bar from the track, ↓ DROP pastes it at the tape head. SHIFT + LIFT erases the track.
Tape tricksIn tape mode hold 1 = brake, 2 = stutter, 3 = reverse.
Shift + RECADVANCED REC (orange light on REC). SOURCE: instrument, mic or both · KEEP OLD: 100% overdubs, 0% replaces what is under the new take, in between fades it · START: on the REC key, ON SOUND (the tape rolls and recording starts when the input passes the threshold) or NEXT BAR · AUTO STOP after 1–8 bars. SHIFT: THRESHOLD · MONITOR (hear the mic) · PUNCH (record only inside the loop markers) · COUNT-IN.
Shift + TAPEERASE TAPE: press T1, T2, T3 and T4 to wipe all four tracks (there is no undo). Shift + TAPE again, or any mode key, cancels before the fourth key.
Shift + 8In tape mode: SYSTEM menu (key 8 is labelled MENU). Blue picks a section, green an item, white changes it: BATTERY · TIME (hour, minute, 12/24 h, date) · TIMEZONE · STORAGE (space used; erase samples, presets, the tape or everything, turning white all the way to YES to confirm) · DISPLAY (brightness, screen sleep, clock in the title bar) · ABOUT.
Shift + T1–T4In tape mode, SHIFT + T1–T4 edit the selected track over the loop (or the bar at the tape head): T1 COPY (then DROP pastes it) · T2 CROP (keep only that part) · T3 DOUBLE (repeat it right after) · T4 ERASE (clear only that part).
SEQSequencers. With the screen open, T1–T4 pick the type. ENDLESS: hold SHIFT and tap notes to add steps, then hold any key to play them transposed. PATTERN: blue knob picks a step, tap keys to toggle notes on it, orange knob starts/stops. ARP: hold a chord. SHIFT + SEQ clears.
CHAINCHAIN (T4): an endless step sequencer. Hold SHIFT and press keys to add steps (keys pressed together make a chord); keep the keys held and press → to make the last note longer; SHIFT + → adds a rest; SHIFT + ← removes the last step. Then press any key to play the chain transposed (the low C plays it as written). Knobs: RATE · GATE · OCTAVE · HOLD.
FXMaster rack: DRIVE → CHORUS → DELAY → REVERB. T1–T4 pick an effect, tap the same T again to switch it on/off. SHIFT + FX switches to bank B: FILTER (DJ low/high-pass with wobble) → GATE (tempo-synced chopper) → FLANGER → LO-FI (bit crush, tape wow and hiss). SHIFT + FX again goes back to bank A.
TempoThe metronome-shaped key opens TEMPO: count-in (green), quantize (white); hold SHIFT for swing, beats and snap. The clock key switches the metronome on/off.
Sampler“load sample” / “record sample” above puts a sound into the current synth slot and plays it across the keys. The knobs are START · END · TUNE · LEVEL; hold SHIFT for PLAY (ONE SHOT plays the whole sample from one tap, GATE plays while the key is held, LOOP loops while it is held) · REVERSE · FADE IN · FADE OUT. An orange line runs through the waveform as each note plays. “clear sample” brings the engine back.
SavingSounds, sequences, samples and the tape are saved on this phone automatically. “export tape as wav” shares the mix.
No sound?Turn the volume up and switch the phone's silent mode off.
Full screenHold the phone sideways. In Safari tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”: OS-1 then opens like an app and fills the screen.

Tap outside the buttons to close.

WEB-1 · CONTROLS

EncodersDrag up/down or scroll over the four coloured knobs. Each matches the same-coloured value on the screen. Double-click resets.

1 – 8Sound slot: 8 synth engines, or 8 drum kits in drum mode. The screen names them A1–A8 (bank B is B1–B8). A3 is STRINGS, a vintage string machine with phaser and cello layer; A7 is HERO, an 80s–90s solo lead with glide and delayed vibrato.
MixerT1 LEVEL & PAN: knobs set track levels, SHIFT + knobs pan them. T2 MULTIBAND COMP: LOW · MID · HIGH amount · MAKEUP; SHIFT: LOW X · HIGH X crossovers · ATTACK · RELEASE. T3 EQ: 4 bands, knobs set the gain, SHIFT + knobs slide the frequency. T4 MASTER: OUT · COMP · INSTR · LIMIT; SHIFT: CEILING · LIM REL · COMP SPD · WIDTH.
Shift + SYNTHSYNTHS & PRESETS menu (Shift + Z): every synth in one list (blue knob) and its presets (green knob); both load as you turn, and the keys still play so you can try them. Shift + SYNTH again or any T key closes it.
Shift + T1In synth mode (or from the SYNTHS & PRESETS menu): SAVE PRESET for the current synth (orange light on T1). Blue knob picks a letter, green moves along the name, turn white all the way right to save, orange all the way right to delete the user preset with that name. On a computer you can also type the name; Enter saves, Esc closes. User presets show with a dot at the end of the synth's list in the menu.
Shift + DRUMDRUM MACHINE (Shift + X): 12 pads built from 2 synth voices and a filter, plus a 16-step pattern. T1 VOICE 1 · T2 VOICE 2 (TUNE, WAVE, DECAY, BEND) · T3 FILTER (MODE, CUTOFF, RESO, MIX) · T4 STEPS (PAD, LENGTH, LEVEL, RUN). Keys play and pick pads; on STEPS the first 16 keys toggle steps (hold Shift to pick a pad instead). Shift + DRUM again closes it; the pattern keeps running.
Pad samplesIn drum mode, hit a pad, then “load sample” / “record sample” (or drop a file) to put a sound on that pad only, for the current kit. The kit’s TUNE and TONE still shape it; Shift + 4 trims it and sets how it plays. “clear sample” brings the built-in sound back.
Shift + 4In drum mode: SAMPLE editor for the selected pad's sample (orange light on 4). START · END · TUNE (±12) · REVERSE; hold SHIFT for PLAY (ONE SHOT plays the whole part from one hit, GATE plays while the key is held, LOOP loops while it is held) · FADE IN · FADE OUT · LEVEL. An orange line runs through the waveform as the pad plays. Hit pads to switch pad; Shift + 4 again or any T key closes it.
Shift + 1 – 8In synth mode: synth bank B (orange light): NEON supersaw · LADDER mono bass/lead with glide · ORBIT poly brass · VELVET warm analog poly pad · BRASS punchy poly brass · AURORA string ensemble · ACID squelch bass (overlap notes to slide) · HOOVER rave lead.
Shift + MSound FX bank B (Shift + T3, orange light on T3): BOUNCE ping-pong tape delay · COIL spring reverb · CUBE feedback-box reverb · RADIO lo-fi radio. Shift + T3 again steps through them; plain T3 goes back to ECHO · ROOM · CRUSH · SWIRL.

IFX RACK: four master effects in a chain, DRIVE → CHORUS → DELAY → REVERB. T1–T4 pick an effect, press the same T again to switch it on/off. Shift+FX (Shift+I) switches to bank B: FILTER (DJ low/high-pass with wobble) → GATE (tempo-synced chopper) → FLANGER → LO-FI (bit crush, tape wow and hiss). Shift+FX (Shift+I) again goes back to bank A. The four lights on the FX key show which are on. Delay is synced to the tempo; delay and reverb tails ring out when switched off. The rack is included in the WAV export.

Count-inOn the TEMPO screen, green knob = COUNT-IN (off / 1 bar / 2 bars). Pressing REC from stop then clicks you in and the tape starts exactly on the downbeat. Press STOP or REC again to cancel.
QuantizeOn the TEMPO screen, white knob = QUANTIZE (off, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32). Notes you play on the keys or over MIDI snap to that grid, which follows the tape's bars; notes only a hair late play straight away. Sequencers start on the grid too. Hold SHIFT on the TEMPO screen for SWING, BEATS and SNAP (loop, lift and drop line up with bars and beats).

Tape: 4 tracks × 60 seconds, variable speed (orange knob in tape mode) — recording at one speed and playing at another shifts pitch, like real tape.
